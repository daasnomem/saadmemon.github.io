// tools_i_built

I'm a musician who builds. Trained in classical and jazz at UMBC, I produce with generative modular workflows, and when a tool I want doesn't exist, I make it. Most of these started as a problem I had as a player.

● LIVE VISUALIZATION
01 · built
Instrument Practice Suite
Separate practice apps for bass, keys and voice. Plug in the real instrument and the app listens: it hears what you play and turns practice into skill games.
  • live instrument input (mic / interface)
  • real-time pitch & timing detection
  • skill games, scoring and streaks
web-audio · pitch-detection · gamified
● LIVE VISUALIZATION
02 · built
ear.io
Gamified ear training in the browser. Intervals, modes and melodies, with XP and ranks so training feels like a game, not homework.
  • modes: learn · time attack · modal · melody
  • 50-melody pool, 3-level hint system
  • additive synthesis on the Web Audio API
web-audio · additive-synthesis · ear-training
● LIVE VISUALIZATION
03 · built
Sample Chopper
A fast tool for slicing and flipping samples. Drop audio in, find the hits, chop it into pads and rearrange it into something new.
  • load audio, detect transients
  • slice to pads, re-sequence
  • built for speed while producing
sampling · transient-detection · web-audio
● LIVE VISUALIZATION
04 · built & performed with
Generative Music + Live Visuals
A generative system for writing and performing. I design the rules, it plays back surprises, and the visuals react live to whatever is being played.
  • rule-based generative sequencing
  • we wrote and performed music with it
  • visuals driven by the audio input
generative · live-performance · audio-reactive
● LIVE VISUALIZATION
05 · live
music.worlds
A personal atlas of how I hear music. My DJ library sorted by feel, not genre: each "world" has its own vocabulary, energy scale and motion.
  • own taxonomy of ~130 tracks
  • paste a link, listen, drag it onto a world
  • the software never auto-classifies
taxonomy · listening · interactive

06 · in development
DIRECTOR
Scenario orchestration for bots inside Counter-Strike 2. The goal: describe a situation in plain words and the bots set it up and play it out. The bot-control layer underneath is working today.
  • C# plugin on CounterStrikeSharp
  • working: navigation, sequencing, handover to combat
  • game systems as a creative medium
game-ai · cs2 · natural-language
